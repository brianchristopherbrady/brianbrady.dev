Bot / scraper / text engine

Our American Gods

An older experimental automation project: scrape two public text sources, combine them through a small rule system, and publish the result on a schedule. It is rougher than the newer work, but it shows an early interest in tooling, language, automation, and systems with a point of view.

View archive

What it did

  • Scrape public topic material from a news source.
  • Scrape and filter source text from a scripture endpoint.
  • Replace repeated phrase structure with current topic language.
  • Publish short generated text through the Twitter API.

Why it belongs

It belongs here because the shape still shows up in later work: take messy source material, define constraints, keep the loop inspectable, and make the output specific enough to feel intentional.
