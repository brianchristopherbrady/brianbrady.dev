Fabric UX and AI-assisted product review

Work around dense product interfaces, design-system constraints, and AI-assisted development workflows. The focus is practical: understand the design, understand the codebase, choose the right component path, and make the next step clear.

What I worked on

  • Review Figma designs against the components and patterns already available in the product.
  • Identify where an existing component fits, where it needs care, and where there is a real system gap.
  • Turn design review into clear implementation guidance instead of vague preference or guesswork.
  • Use AI workflows to gather evidence, compare options, and keep decisions tied to product reality.

Why it matters

Enterprise UI work is rarely about making a screen in isolation. It is about respecting what already exists, keeping repeated workflows predictable, and making sure design intent survives the implementation path.
