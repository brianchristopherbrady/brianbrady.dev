Enterprise UX / product surfaces

Fabric UX

Product UI work for dense enterprise surfaces: component parity, review iteration, accessibility expectations, and interaction details that have to hold up under repeated use.

What matters here

  • Migration paths that respect existing product behavior.
  • Clear decisions around component fit, composition, and interaction parity.
  • Dense UI that stays scannable and predictable.
  • Review discipline across active and abandoned branches.

Constraint set

The interesting work is often in the constraints: design-system availability, accessibility behavior, product history, and the difference between what a component can render and what a workflow actually needs.
